<objective>
Escalate HSTS from 5-minute staging through 6-month to 12-month max-age, enable the preload directive, and submit unth.ai to the browser HSTS preload list.

Purpose: HSTS preload is the final and strongest form of HTTP→HTTPS enforcement — it prevents even the first HTTP request by hardcoding unth.ai in browsers. This must be staged because preload is effectively permanent for the life of the max-age duration. The plan runs in two sub-sessions: Task 1 runs 24h after Plan 03; Tasks 2-3 run 1 week after Plan 03.

Output: HSTS at 12 months + preload flag, submitted to hstspreload.org, phase complete.
</objective>
